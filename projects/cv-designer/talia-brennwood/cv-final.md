## Professional Summary

Product designer working since 2017 across healthcare, B2B SaaS and public transit, focused on forms, workflows, accessibility and design systems. At Fernhollow Health, a telehealth company, improved patient intake completion, co-built the Fern UI design system adopted across product teams, and led an accessibility remediation to WCAG 2.1 AA in a regulated healthcare setting. Mentors 2 mid-level designers, facilitates the weekly design critique and gave a 2023 talk at Cascadia UX Meetup on intake forms.

## Experience

### Senior Product Designer | Fernhollow Health | 2022 - Present

- Redesigned patient intake for a telehealth product, raising completion rate from 61% to 84% over about 2 quarters.
- Co-built the Fern UI design system with 1 other designer and 3 engineers; now used by 5 product teams.
- Led an accessibility audit and remediation to reach WCAG 2.1 AA on the web app, coordinating with legal.
- Partnered with clinicians to design provider-side appointment scheduling (reminders, easier rescheduling), with no-shows down 18%.
- Facilitate the weekly design critique and mentor 2 mid-level designers (one has been promoted).

### Product Designer | Quillmark | June 2018 - February 2022

- Designed approval workflows for enterprise customers, contributing to product-line growth from 2M to 9M ARR.
- Designed the iOS and Android mobile app v1 as the sole designer.
- Redesigned the onboarding flow.
- Conducted about 40 customer interviews per year and set up the research repository in Dovetail.

- Redesigned the public-website trip planner, raising usability-test task success from 58% to 89%.
