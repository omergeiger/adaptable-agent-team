## Slice 6 selection-rule change: GO
Pre-mortem of the change:
- Hard floor of 3 on interest-match: GOOD, removes the "floor lowered to 1" rung that previously let ALIVE/PDF-type noise through. Correct direction; shipping 3 good items beats padding.
- Risk A: floor is on interest-match, which is exactly the inflated signal (see above). Items 1 and 2 passed it with 5. The floor cannot catch keyword false positives. Condition 1: treat a lone generic keyword ("agent", "agents", "llm", "ai") as a weak match (score <=2) unless a second distinct keyword or a title/topic phrase hits. Under that rule item 1 drops out, item 2 likely too.
- Risk B: confirmed-low interests (AI safety, rust) and inferred cannot ship in main slots. This is a narrowing; with the floor at 3 and weights 1-2 these can only ever appear via exploration, and exploration also min 3, so they effectively vanish. Mitigation: allow exploration slot to accept in-interest weight 1-2 confirmed items at min 3 on a hands-on test (keeps rust/safety alive). Condition 2: verify this is what happens, or Alex's stated "sometimes: rust" dies by construction.
- Risk C: best-effort source diversity with a warning only. Acceptable, but warnings must not be the only guard; QA should alarm if 3 consecutive editions are single-source-type.
- Risk D: parallel link checks and "blocked/ssl kept unverified" - unverified links may ship as dead. Require them to be marked in the sidecar, not hidden. Low severity.
- "Not measured" live runtime: not a blocker for a critic GO; QA owns it.
