## Standing Directives

D1. Focus area is AI tooling (developer tools, agent frameworks, model APIs/SDKs, coding assistants, evals/observability, AI infra for builders).
D2. Rank by significance, not popularity: virality, upvotes and social buzz are not evidence of importance.
D3. Every published item must link to a primary source.
D4. Never repeat an item covered in the last 7 digests. A real follow-up (e.g. a GA after a preview) counts as new only if the digest says what changed.
D5. Skip funding-round news unless the round is over $100M.
D6. Exactly 5 items per digest. If fewer than 5 significant items exist, publish fewer and say so; never pad.
D7. Only items published in the last 24 hours before the cycle time.
