## Standing Directives

D1. ATS-safe formatting only - no tables, columns, text boxes, images, or decorative elements in any version submitted to an ATS. The `cv-final-ats.txt` file is the reference for ATS submission.
D2. When Design is complete, the orchestrator posts "CV ready for sign-off: [candidate name]. To approve, add `- [x] approve [candidate-slug]` under `## Approvals` in human_directives.md." to Open Questions and does NOT write `cv-final.md` until the human adds that line to `## Approvals` below.
D3. No fabrication - every claim, date, credential, and metric must trace to the human's source material in `intake/raw-material.md`. Agents must flag gaps rather than fill them.
D4. Export to PDF within the loop; the PDF must fit on one page.
